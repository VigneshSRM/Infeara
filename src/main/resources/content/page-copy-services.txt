title: Cybersecurity Services, Audits & Testing | Infeara
description: Security audits, vulnerability assessment and penetration testing, identity reviews, and perimeter checks, scoped with written authorization first.
intro: Security work starts with an accurate picture of what is exposed and how it is controlled. We agree scope and rules of engagement in writing, test within that boundary, and report findings in a form your team can act on.
covers: Vulnerability assessment and penetration testing (VAPT) | Network, application, and cloud security audits | Endpoint, firewall, and perimeter configuration reviews | Identity, access, and privileged-account reviews
signs: Security work is directed by vendor alerts rather than a risk view of your own environment | Access rights have accumulated over time and nobody owns the review | Assurance is needed for a client, insurer, or internal audit
deliverables: A findings register ordered by risk, with the evidence behind each item | Remediation guidance that separates quick fixes from structural work | A short walkthrough with your team so the report is understood, not just filed

faq: Will an audit disrupt our team? | Most audit work is read-only configuration review that runs alongside normal operations. Anything requiring a change window, a test account, or user involvement is scheduled with you first.
