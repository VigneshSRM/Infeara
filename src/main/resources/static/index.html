07GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might be wondering.

Still have a question? We’d be glad to hear it.

Talk to our team ↗
Who do you work with?

We work with businesses looking for a thoughtful technology partner—whether you need help with a defined project, ongoing IT operations, or a clearer view of what to do next.

Can I ask about just one service?

Absolutely. You can come to us with a focused need, such as a network review or infrastructure project. We’ll learn about your requirements and discuss the right scope together.

How do you approach cybersecurity assessments?

Security work should always be clearly authorized and scoped in writing. Before an assessment, we align on systems in scope, rules of engagement, data handling, deliverables, and any retesting expectations.

Do you work with a specific technology vendor?

We consider the needs of your environment and the fit of the solution. Any vendor experience, formal partnership, or certification should be confirmed for the specific engagement.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

Your message is shared with Infeara so the team can understand what you need and follow up. Include the best way to reach you and a little context to help us prepare.
