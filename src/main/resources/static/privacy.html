Privacy notice.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This notice explains how Infeara Technologies handles information you choose to provide through the consultation form on this website.

Information you provide

If you submit an enquiry, the form asks for your name, business email, company, service of interest, and a description of what you need. Role or department and preferred contact method are optional. The website also records the page from which the enquiry was sent.

How we use it

We use the information to understand and respond to your enquiry, communicate with you about a possible engagement, and maintain appropriate records of business enquiries. We do not sell the information submitted through this form.

Storage and access

Enquiries are stored in a database service operated for Infeara Technologies. Server-side credentials and database access policies restrict access; enquiry records are not publicly readable. Enquiry records are kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond and maintain appropriate business records. To request deletion, use the privacy-request option below; requests are reviewed and handled in line with applicable requirements.

Your choices and requests

You may choose not to provide optional information. If you would like to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, use the contact form, select “Privacy request,” and describe your request. Applicable rights and response processes depend on the law that applies to your circumstances.

Third-party website resources

This website currently requests typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser loads those fonts, Google may receive the request and related technical information such as your IP address. See Google’s privacy information for details. The site does not currently include analytics or advertising scripts. If those tools are added later, this notice will be updated to describe them accurately.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the website and its enquiry process change. Please review this page periodically for current information.
