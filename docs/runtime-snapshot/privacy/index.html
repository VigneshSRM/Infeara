INFEARA TECHNOLOGIES / WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This notice explains how Infeara Technologies handles information you choose to provide through this website.

Information you provide

If you submit a consultation enquiry, the form asks for your name, business email, company, service of interest, and a description of what you need. Role or department and preferred contact method are optional. The website records the page from which the enquiry was sent.

How we use it

We use the information to understand and respond to your enquiry, communicate about a possible engagement, and maintain appropriate records of business enquiries. We do not sell information submitted through this form.

Storage and retention

Enquiries are stored in a database service operated for Infeara Technologies. Server-side credentials and database access policies restrict access; enquiry records are not publicly readable. Enquiry records are kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond and maintain appropriate business records. To request deletion, use the privacy-request option below; requests are reviewed and handled in line with applicable requirements.

Your choices and requests

You may choose not to provide optional information. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, use the contact form, select “Privacy request,” and describe your request. Applicable rights depend on the law that applies to your circumstances.

Third-party resources

This website requests typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser loads them, Google may receive the request and related technical information such as your IP address. See Google’s privacy information. This website does not currently include analytics or advertising scripts.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the website and enquiry process change. Please review this page periodically for the current information.
