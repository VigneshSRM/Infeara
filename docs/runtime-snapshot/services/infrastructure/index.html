03COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about
IT infrastructure services.

Can you work with our existing hardware?

Yes. We start from an inventory of what is installed and supported, then recommend changes only where they remove a real constraint, such as end-of-support hardware or a capacity ceiling.

Do you replace our IT team?

No. Most engagements either add capacity alongside your team or take ownership of specific layers, such as server administration or backup verification, while your team keeps the rest.
