CYBERSECURITY / INTELLIGENCE & RECOVERYSCOPED / PRACTICAL / DOCUMENTED

Threat intelligence and disaster recovery audits

Threat context tells you what to expect; recovery testing tells you what you can survive. This work connects the two.

Discuss this service ↗
01 / 02
SCOPE / EXAMPLES

Clear scope.
Responsible work.

01Threat landscape and relevant indicator review
02Backup coverage and restore feasibility review
03Recovery time and recovery point validation
04Incident response and escalation readiness review

Security testing is performed only with written authorization and a documented scope, rules of engagement, data-handling expectations, and reporting plan. No testing begins before approval.

02SERVICE DETAIL

What usually prompts this work.

  • 01Recovery plans exist but have never been exercised
  • 02Backup success is reported without restore tests
  • 03The threat picture is generic rather than specific to your sector

What you receive.

  • 01A current exposure view with sector-relevant scenarios
  • 02Restore test results for representative systems
  • 03A recovery gap list with sequencing and ownership
03COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about
Threat intelligence and disaster recovery audits.

Do you run a full disaster recovery exercise?

We scope a representative test: a subset of critical systems, a defined scenario, and named participants. Full-scale exercises are planned around business tolerance for downtime.

How is threat intelligence used?

As prioritization input, not as a product. It shapes which risks are examined first and which scenarios are tested.
